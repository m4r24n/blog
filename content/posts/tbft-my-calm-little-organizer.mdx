The idea of TBFT is a private, shared workspace. I’ve been building it for me and my partner, who lives in a continent apart. Even though she doesn't care about it much, I have been very inspired. It has happened a few times in my life that I got inspired from my own projects.

![Article image](/images/posts/tbft-my-calm-little-organizer/media-tbft.png)

Anyway, The main ideas are pretty simple:

We can both see what the other one doing.
My stuff is on my side, your stuff is on yours, and we both know what’s going on.
Big things get their own little home.
If we’re working on something bigger, like a thesis or a trip, it gets its own project space where the tasks and files stay together.
Files don’t get lost in Google Drive.
TBFT makes the folders for us and keeps the right files connected to the right task or project.
Some jobs can repeat themselves.
If something needs doing every week or every month, TBFT remembers. So every day when I open my dashboard, I see assigned tasks, right when I need it.
Deleting something doesn’t instantly destroy it.
Things can sit safely in the archive first, and only disappear for real when we actually mean it.
It can look like us.

The whole idea is pretty much this: a calm little shared space for two people trying to keep life together without making life feel like a mess.
